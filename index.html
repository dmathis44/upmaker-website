’cause the bus ain’t coming!

UpMaker is the listing machine for car salespeople. It pulls your dealership’s website inventory and turns it into Facebook Marketplace listings — with your name and your number on them. You already photographed every car for the website. Stop doing the job twice.

How it works

Website inventory in. Marketplace listings out.

1

Sync your whole lot

One tap syncs your store’s website inventory into the app — every unit that isn’t already in your app, every sync, with photos, mileage, and pricing pulled for you.

2

Prepare the listing

UpMaker serves up Facebook’s listing form in Facebook’s exact order — media, location, year, make, model, mileage, price, body style, colors, title, condition, fuel, transmission — so posting is tap and paste, not twenty questions.

3

Your name on it

Every photo gets your name and your cell number baked in on a call-or-text strip, and the app builds a listing video for the vehicle — title and price banner, contact end card, ready to post.

4

Post it. Work the replies.

A one-tap handoff sends the photos, video, and listing text to Marketplace. When buyers message, Pro drafts your replies — tuned to book appointments — and you hit send.

Features

Built for the way salespeople actually work

Locked, honest pricing

Prices are website-driven and locked in the app, so what’s advertised always matches the site. Your doc fee is baked into every advertised price, with tax, title, and license called out — FTC-safe by construction.

One-tap website sync

New arrivals appear as drafts with their website photos and specs. Price changes on the site are caught and applied automatically. No re-typing VINs, no re-uploading photos.

Works across dealer platforms

UpMaker reads structured inventory data from the major dealer website platforms, with fallbacks for the rest — if your store has a website with inventory on it, UpMaker can sync it.

Branded photos

Your name and your cell number on every photo, baked in before posting — so when a buyer is ready, they call you, not the store’s main line.

Listing video, built in

A slideshow listing video for every vehicle — title and price banner, a scrolling call-or-text ticker, and a contact end card — generated right in the app.

One-tap Marketplace handoff

Photos, video, and pre-formatted listing text move to Facebook Marketplace in one tap, with a step-by-step posting checklist built for Marketplace. You review it and you publish it.

Pro: Messenger reply drafts

Smart draft replies by message category — price, availability, trade-in, financing, test drive. It drafts, you hit send. Your words, your relationships, your appointments.

Pro: VIN barcode scanner

Scan the VIN barcode on the door jamb and get the decoded vehicle — no fat-fingering a 17-character VIN on the lot again.

Local-first and private

Your inventory, photos, and settings live on your device. No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no data sold or shared. See our privacy policy.
